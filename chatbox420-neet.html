What is NEET UG?

NEET UG is the single national entrance test for undergraduate medical and allied courses in India. Your score and rank decide which MBBS, BDS or AYUSH seat you can get through counselling.

NEET UG eligibility

  • Passed or appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English.
  • Minimum age of 17 years on or before 31 December of the admission year.
  • Minimum marks in Physics, Chemistry and Biology are set by category (commonly 50% General, 40% reserved categories, 45% for General-PwD). Confirm in the bulletin.

NEET syllabus at a glance

  • Physics: mechanics, heat, electricity and magnetism, optics, modern physics.
  • Chemistry: physical, organic and inorganic chemistry, with heavy NCERT focus.
  • Botany: plant diversity, plant physiology, genetics, ecology.
  • Zoology: human physiology, reproduction, evolution, biotechnology.

All topics come from NCERT Class 11 and 12. Read the official syllabus on the NTA website.

chatbox420 NEET study plan

  1. Biology first: it carries half the paper. Read NCERT line by line, then practise questions on every chapter.
  2. Chemistry next: master NCERT, then add problem practice for physical chemistry and reaction mechanisms.
  3. Physics steadily: build concepts, then practise numericals daily so calculations stay quick.
  4. Last 4–6 months: weekly full-length mocks with OMR practice, plus revision of weak chapters.

chatbox420 tips to score higher

  • Revise NCERT diagrams, tables and exceptions in Biology — they are favourite exam areas.
  • Solve previous years' papers and note which chapters repeat.
  • Avoid risky guessing because of negative marking.
  • Train your speed: about one minute per question on average.
  • Protect sleep and health in the final month.

Counselling and seats

MCC conducts counselling for all-India quota seats, deemed and central universities and some other institutes, while state authorities handle state-quota seats. Seats are limited compared with the number of candidates, so keep backup paths such as BDS, AYUSH and allied health sciences in mind. Details: mcc.nic.in.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NEET UG exam pattern?

In the recent format the paper has 180 compulsory multiple-choice questions for 720 marks: 45 each in Physics and Chemistry and 90 in Biology, with +4 for correct and −1 for incorrect answers. Check the current bulletin for the exact mode and duration.

Is NCERT enough for NEET?

NCERT is essential and covers most of Biology and Chemistry. Physics needs additional numerical practice. chatbox420 recommends NCERT first, then practice questions and mock tests.

How many attempts are allowed in NEET UG?

There is no limit on the number of attempts under the current rules, as long as you meet the eligibility conditions.

Is NEET compulsory for MBBS?

Yes. Admission to MBBS in India, in government, private and deemed colleges, is based on the NEET UG score.

Where can I find official NEET information?

On neet.nta.nic.in for the exam and mcc.nic.in for counselling. chatbox420 simplifies the basics, but official notices always take priority.

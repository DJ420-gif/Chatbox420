What is JEE?

JEE is India's main entrance route to engineering. JEE Main is the first stage and the gateway to NITs, IIITs and many other institutes. Top performers in JEE Main can also appear in JEE Advanced, which decides admission to the IITs.

Both exams test Class 11 and 12 Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, but JEE Advanced goes much deeper into concepts and problem solving.

JEE syllabus at a glance

  • Physics: mechanics, heat and thermodynamics, waves, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, optics, modern physics.
  • Chemistry: physical (mole concept, equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry), inorganic (periodic table, bonding, coordination compounds) and organic (reaction mechanisms, functional groups, biomolecules).
  • Mathematics: algebra, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, calculus, vectors and 3D geometry, probability.

The syllabus is based on NCERT Class 11 and 12 with extra problem-solving depth. Download the official syllabus from the NTA website.

chatbox420 JEE study plan

  1. Foundation (Class 11 and early Class 12): finish NCERT, learn each concept properly and solve basic questions daily.
  2. Practice: solve topic-wise problems, raise the difficulty step by step and keep an error notebook.
  3. Revision and mocks (last 4–6 months): take a full-length mock every week, analyse every mistake and revise formulas daily.

A simple daily rhythm from chatbox420: one concept block, one practice block, one revision block, and enough sleep to remember it all.

chatbox420 tips to score higher

  • Accuracy first: wrong answers carry negative marking, so avoid blind guessing.
  • Attempt in rounds: easy questions first, then medium, then hard.
  • Do not skip Chemistry NCERT: it is the quickest scoring subject for many students.
  • Use past papers: practise previous years' questions under timed conditions.
  • Limit your resources: one good book per subject, revised many times, beats ten books read once.

After the exam: ranks and counselling

Seats in IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs are allotted through JoSAA counselling based on your rank. Keep your documents ready, research branches and colleges in advance, and fill your choices in order of real preference. Official details are on josaa.nic.in.

Frequently asked questions

How many attempts are allowed in JEE Main?

Under the current rules, a candidate can appear for JEE Main for up to three consecutive years starting from the year of passing Class 12, with two sessions each year. Check the latest official bulletin for any update.

Is NCERT enough for JEE Main?

NCERT is the foundation, especially for Chemistry, but Physics and Mathematics need extra problem practice. The chatbox420 approach is NCERT first, then topic-wise practice, then mock tests.

Is there negative marking in JEE Main?

Yes. Incorrect answers lose marks (−1 under the current pattern) and unattempted questions score zero, so accuracy matters. Confirm the exact rules for numerical questions in the official bulletin.

When should I start JEE preparation?

Starting in Class 11 gives the most comfortable runway, but a focused plan can work for Class 12 students and droppers too. Consistency matters more than the start date.

Where can I read the official JEE notification?

On jeemain.nta.nic.in for JEE Main and jeeadv.ac.in for JEE Advanced. chatbox420 summarises the basics, but the official bulletin always has the final word.
